# What CaseOh reacts to in sim games
Date: 2026-10-21

**How this was made:** I listed sim videos on CaseOh's channel (@caseoh_) and pulled the auto-captions for 13 videos across 9 games. I did not download any video or audio. Captions only hold what he says, so his reactions are clear, but NPC voice lines and game sounds are mostly missing (anything said about audio is a **guess**). CAPS in the captions = him yelling, which I used as the "big reaction" signal. Timestamps are about ±30 s.

This file does not repeat [sim-games.md](sim-games.md), [sim-games-2.md](sim-games-2.md) or [polish-and-next.md](polish-and-next.md). Those are about systems and Steam reviews. This one is about **what makes a moment worth watching**.

## 1. Per game: loop, big moments, complaints

### Waterpark Simulator (closest to us)
- **Loop:** clear a ruined lot, then build a pool, slide and loungers from a build menu. Sell tickets at the booth (pick hours, age and slide pass), then run out to mop puddles, pick up litter and poop, check wristbands and save guests who are drowning. After a shift, spend research points and money, and hand out flyers in town.
- **Biggest moments:**
  - **Naming the park with chat** takes about 8 minutes before any gameplay. Chat votes in two rounds and "Goob Lagoon" wins ([ep1 2:33](https://youtu.be/K7j8I6cNyjY?t=153)).
  - **Ticket cheater:** a guest with no wristband gets chased out and slips on a puddle on his way out. He calls it the funniest thing he has ever seen ([ep1 32:30](https://youtu.be/K7j8I6cNyjY?t=1950)).
  - **Drowning guest:** the gargling, then a fumbled life-ring rescue. He calls it the best game he has ever played ([ep1 59:52](https://youtu.be/K7j8I6cNyjY?t=3592)).
  - **Guests hurting themselves** on slides (head-first, backwards) and a buggy "death loop" slide that becomes the guests' favourite. He keeps it because it is funny ([ep21 9:09](https://youtu.be/k3ptkJDtOvQ?t=549)).
  - **Meltdown shift:** trash in the pools, overflowing bins, puke, slips, long lines, and he says he's about to panic. Then the report says it was his **best shift ever**. The gap between how it felt and what the report says is the joke ([ep10 19:08 to 23:08](https://youtu.be/L8FFv4bmtXw?t=1148)).
  - **Named staff become characters:** River, the cashier who waves at nothing, gets fired mid-shift. He then hires Kaiser by comparing stat cards ([ep10 69:56](https://youtu.be/L8FFv4bmtXw?t=4196)).
  - **He tests every new slide himself first**, then begs guests to try it. They keep using the old death loop instead ([ep21 35:39](https://youtu.be/k3ptkJDtOvQ?t=2139)).
  - **5 stars** gets a triumphant speech and unlocks luxury content ([ep21 12:50](https://youtu.be/k3ptkJDtOvQ?t=770)).
- **Complaints:**
  - "Waited too long for a ticket" while he was saving someone.
  - A full inventory when he needed the life ring.
  - Staff on break or idle while the line grows.
  - Guests walking into his tools.
  - Showers that broke without him noticing.
- **End of day:**
  - Guests leave and a "closing in 20 seconds" notice shows ([ep10 51:13](https://youtu.be/L8FFv4bmtXw?t=3073)).
  - Then a report: guest count, satisfaction %, how many slipped / were bored / got hurt, and money. He argues with every line.
  - Then build time, and he sleeps in a tent beside the park.

## 2. Patterns that make a sim streamer-friendly

1. **Naming is content.** 5 of 9 games opened with a naming segment with chat (1.5-13 min; Waterpark ~8, Fast Food ~7, Theater ~13, TCG ~3, Schedule I ~1.5 plus ~6 in the character creator), often with a poll and a final vote. Supermarket added a paid mid-series rename. Gas Station and Kebab had none; the Laundry video was not episode 1. The name is then used for jokes all series ("goobertastic"). It costs almost nothing to build.
2. **NPCs he can judge.** The loudest reactions are to people, not money:
   - cheaters (no wristband), freeloaders (free laundry, the trash-can guy), vandals (Dennis);
   - the smelly guy, the loiterer who never buys, the person who arrives at 8:59;
   - the "searching an empty shelf" animation.
   He needs a **villain to throw out** and a **hero customer to celebrate** (best customer ever, the Platinum Club).
3. **Names make recurring characters.** Kurt, River, Kaiser, Tim, Dennis, Jimmy: a named NPC who comes back becomes a running joke across episodes. A nameless NPC is forgotten.
4. **Physical slapstick beats numbers.** Slips on puddles, guests flying off slides, drowning-then-rescue, the boss breaking his legs on his own high dive. These got bigger CAPS than any money milestone.
5. **Rush = "suffering from success".** A sudden line he can't handle, followed by a report that says it went great. Chaos must be survivable, and it must end.
6. **Random big wins.** TCG pulls, the $700 card finally selling, a rich customer, a $2 can. Uncertain rewards are the main reason he keeps going.
7. **Readable complaints he can argue with.** "Too expensive" bubbles, written reviews he reads aloud, satisfaction lines in the report. Every complaint is a line he can answer, but only if it says **why**.
8. **Test the new thing yourself, then wait for "the first" guest.** Buying a slide or machine is half the payoff. The other half is watching who uses it first, or that nobody does.
9. **The end-of-day ritual is the same every day:**
   - closing call;
   - late stragglers;
   - clean up;
   - **walk around collecting coins**;
   - report with funny stat lines;
   - bills or loan, and a chat vote;
   - spend on upgrades;
   - sleep.
   It gives every episode a natural ending and a "one more day" hook.
10. **Story calls with stakes.** The uncle's debt in Gas Station and the gang note in Schedule I were peak moments. One surprise every few days beats a steady drip.
11. **Chat decides.** Name polls, loan votes, layout, "type 1 for here". A game that offers **two-option decisions** gives the streamer a ready-made poll.
12. **Sound (mostly a guess, captions can't hear it):** he imitated the gargle of a drowning guest and reacted to the counter bell, honking and loud music. Things to take from that:
    - Short readable cues: bell, gargle, register, whistle.
    - Music quieter than voices. He complained about loud music twice.

**What kills the fun (avoid):**
- tasks that get stuck;
- unclear staff idling;
- the same button for drop and pick-up;
- a full inventory when you need an emergency item;
- a close button that throws out paying guests;
- unclear profit, no clock or map;
- constant restocking with no "restock all";
- guests walking into your tools.

## 3. Ideas for our surf park (ranked)
Size: S = under 1 session, M = 1 session, L = 2-3 sessions. I checked the code first: there is no park naming, no surfer names, no written reviews, no sneak-ins or ejecting, and `DayCycle.EndDay` pauses straight into the report at 8 PM.

**Why these three are on top:**
- #1 costs a session and gives every playthrough a personal hook.
- #2 turns faceless surfers into characters and makes the report worth reading aloud. #4, #5, #12 and #15 all build on it.
- #3 replaces an abrupt pause with the loop every sim on the list has: last guests leave, you walk round collecting coins, then you read the report.

**Avoid list for us** (from section 1 complaints; tick these off before streamers find them):
- Separate keys for drop and pick-up.
- A clock always on screen.
- Never close on paying guests: `DayCycle` must let current sessions finish.
- Show why staff are idle.
- No stuck missions: every objective needs a fallback.
- Music under voices.
- Rescue tools never blocked by a full hand.
- NPCs step around the player's tools.
