## 3. Why they stick (what press/players say)
- **Funny from the body, not detail.** Fall Guys style: the body is bad at the job, so every mistake is a joke ([character-look.md](character-look.md) Gang Beasts/Fall Guys row). Gang Beasts' simple look is credited with making it easy for streamers to adopt [S: MCV]. How to Fish's wins are "player-made moments" like gambling the team's money or a trick shot, not model quality [S: Player.One]. Cheap graphics did not stop an Overwhelmingly Positive rating [S].
- **Faces as the cosmetic.** In PEAK, eyes and mouth are things you swap and unlock [S]; Content Warning lets you type the face [S]; R.E.P.O. moders even change pupil size [S]. A face that is a few swappable parts is cheap AND a reward system.
- **Mocked/risky:** Schedule I's faces are called too close to Rick and Morty [S, fan opinion]. Lesson: a strong borrowed face style gets noticed, so put our own twist on it (surf zinc, tan, sunglasses). Gang Beasts sphere hands make grips unreadable [S]; keep our mittens readable with a thumb.
- **Physics wobble = life.** PEAK scouts ragdoll, arms twist, ghosts stare at you [S]. [G] This shared "floppy" feel is a big part of why people share clips.

## 4. Cheap technical tricks for Unity URP (all [G]/[Idea], standard techniques)
1. **Face as texture decal on a smooth sphere/rounded head.** One small atlas (e.g. 4x4 cells: eyes open/blink/happy/scared, mouths smile/grin/O/flat), one material "Face", swap by changing UV offset via `MaterialPropertyBlock` (no extra draw calls beyond one material per head). Cheaper than 8 meshes per face, and eyes can be big and clean at any size. Cost: needs a UV-mapped head and a tiny shader/Shader Graph (we already use Shader Graph for water).
2. **Layered faces (Schedule I style):** brows, eyes, mouth, blush as separate quads/decal layers with their own tint. That is also what our Blender meshes do, just as geometry.
3. **Geometry faces (ours today):** fine up close, but many tiny meshes per person = many draw calls with 40 NPCs. Fix by merging one "face" mesh per swap (all brows/eyes/mouth variants in one mesh using blend shapes or submeshes) or by switching to decals for NPCs far away.
4. **Emissive / unlit eyes:** make eye whites + pupils unlit or slightly emissive so they pop in shade and under sunset light. URP Unlit or Lit with an emission color; cheap.
5. **Vertex colors for outfit bands:** painting bands in vertex colors (shader reads them) means one material for the whole outfit; tint by a property block. Our house notes already lean this way.
6. **Outline:** URP renderer feature "inverted hull" (second pass, back faces pushed out) costs one more draw per body; skip for crowds, use only for the player's hands/mirror or hover highlight. None of the games above are confirmed to use outlines [G].
7. **Fresnel rim light:** a few lines in Shader Graph (Fresnel Effect node * sunset color) gives a warm edge that separates a character from the water. Cheap and fits our "Sunset Surf Shop" look [Idea].
8. **Wobble:** secondary motion without full ragdoll: spring-damped bone offsets for hair, head, arms (a small `SpringBone` script), plus full ragdoll only on wipeout. PEAK/Gang Beasts do real physics [S], but we can fake it: surfers already follow a path, so full physics would fight our wave code.
9. **Blob shadow / contact shadow** under feet so mitten feet look grounded (cheap decal) [G].

## 6. Ranked recommendations (S/M/L) [Idea]
1. **Keep the proportions and swappable face parts (S):** nothing here says change them. Do not adopt Schedule I's realistic body; it is too close to Rick and Morty for us.
2. **Face unlock rewards (S-M, Step 4+):** earn eyes/mouths/brows as challenge rewards (like PEAK badges [S]): zinc nose, cat mouth, monster eyes for Halloween. Reuses mouth swap code.
3. **Quirky face and blink, plus a face for each state (S):** PEAK has an unconscious face [S]; we add a dizzy face for wipeouts and a sleepy face for the queue. Mostly texture/mesh swap in `CharacterRig`.
4. **Unlit/emissive eyes and a warm Fresnel rim on bodies (S):** the best look-per-hour change; protects faces in evening shade.
5. **Spring wobble on hair, head, arms and hat (M):** fake physics, not ragdoll; strong "alive" feel, great for clips. Lethal rule: keep wave path logic untouched.
6. **Ragdoll wipeout on the player and NPC (M-L):** the classic funny moment (How to Fish seagull gag, PEAK fall [S]); switch from animation to ragdoll only on the fall, then blend back. Needs a ragdoll collider setup per rig, risky with water, so prototype on land first.
7. **Decal face atlas for NPCs far away (M):** only if the geometry faces cost too much (40+ NPCs); otherwise keep geometry. Check the Frame Debugger draw-call count first.
8. **Player character creator in the locker room (L):** PEAK's Airport passport and Schedule I's phone screen show customization is a hook [S]; we have the kit already. Needs UI Toolkit screen + save.
9. **Inverted-hull outline (M):** only for selection highlight or customizer; low priority.
