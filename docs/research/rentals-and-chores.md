# Board rentals and carry/restock chores
Date: 2026-10-20

Tags: [FACT] = from a source, [GUESS] = my guess or memory, not checked.

## 1. Real board rentals

### What is rented
- Wave pools rent three groups: **softboards** (foam "foamies"), **hardboards** (fiberglass/epoxy), and **wetsuits**. Some also rent leashes, handplanes, bodyboards.
- Lost Shore (UK) price list: standard softboards free with a lesson/session (7'0" fun, 8'0" mini-mal, 9'0" long); hybrid softboards GBP 15; standard hardboards 20; premium hardboards 25; specialty boards 30. A multi-session rate caps the daily fee. [FACT] [Lost Shore](https://www.lostshore.com/surf/board-hire/)
- Munich surf park: hardboards EUR 40/session, softboards EUR 20. [FACT] [Wave Pool Mag](https://wavepoolmag.com/managing-gear-and-retail-wave-pool/)
- Foamies are free at most wave pools; The Wave (Bristol) went free, then GBP 10, then a flat GBP 25 for its premium boards. [FACT] [Wave Pool Mag](https://wavepoolmag.com/?p=12214)
- Surf-school rentals (not pools) are far cheaper: about EUR 15 for 2 h softboard, +EUR 5 wetsuit. [FACT] [9Pies](https://www.escueladesurf9pies.com/en/cursos/rentals), [Hart Beach](https://www.hartbeach.nl/english/surf-school/rentals/surfboards.html)
- US day rates for a soft-top: roughly 15-40 USD (low-quality cost guide). [FACT, weak] [guide](https://new.risingsunartscentre.org/news/surfboard-rental-cost-guide-2026.html)
- Texas pool BSR: one session about 90 USD; sells everything from soft tops to performance shortboards. [FACT, old] [Surf Park Central](https://surfparkcentral.com/surf-trip-travel-guide-bsr-surf-resort-waco-texas/)

### Rental flow
1. Staff ask about skill ("when did you last surf?") and match a board; beginners get big soft boards. [FACT] [Wave Pool Mag](https://wavepoolmag.com/?p=12214)
2. **Deposit**: Lost Shore holds your photo ID until the board is back. [FACT]
3. Report any dings before entering the water; damaged gear can't be used. [FACT]
4. Late return (45+ min) costs an extra period. [FACT]
5. Lost/stolen: pay full market value. Returned damaged: GBP 10 fee. [FACT]
6. Waiver: surfers sign one for the session anyway (standard practice, I didn't find a rental-specific source). [GUESS]
7. Leash is required in BSR's pool. [FACT]

### Damage, fleet size, upkeep
- Typical damage: rail dings from bailed takeoffs, snapped noses, fin plugs, wall scrapes. [FACT] [Wave Pool Mag](https://wavepoolmag.com/?p=12214)
- The Wave inspected boards and riders before/after each session, then moved to a flat price with a damage fee baked in, and built an on-site **repair bay**. They chose tougher boards (Firewire, Lib Tech). [FACT]
- Repair prices (Australian hire shop): ding 70-90, nose crack 110-145, snapped board 250-320 AUD. [FACT] [Southern Man](https://www.southernman.com.au/hire-repair-pricelist/)
- Fleet examples: The Wave about 56 hardboards; Munich 70-80 boards plus 250-300 wetsuits in use (1,800 in storage). [FACT]
- Wetsuits tear at neck and sleeve ends, mostly from beginners pulling wrong; thickness changes by season (5/3 winter, shorty summer). [FACT]
- Cleaning/waxing: I found no source. Normal practice is wax before use and rinse/hang-dry suits. [GUESS]
- No source for damage rates per board. [GAP]

### What a wave-pool shop sells
- Surfboards and fins, sunscreen, caps, sunglasses (best on hot days), towels (sales rose when moved to the counter), branded merch. [FACT] [Wave Pool Mag](https://wavepoolmag.com/managing-gear-and-retail-wave-pool/)
- Lesson: "go easy on opening orders"; mid-length boards rented far more than expected. [FACT]
- Rent-then-buy-with-discount links the rental desk to the shop. [FACT]

## 2. Why carry/restock chores work (or don't)

Sources are mostly Steam threads and guides, so treat as player opinion.

### Supermarket Simulator
- Restocking is the #1 grind complaint: one player says refilling takes about 30 min real time while the shop empties it in about 5. [FACT] [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2670630/discussions/0/4413047527584688437)
- Players ask for automatic ordering. [FACT]
- A **restocker employee** unlocks at a level; it refills shelves and bins empty boxes, after which you "just monitor and order". [FACT] [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2670630/discussions/0/4342103279866152901), [guide](https://commonsensegamer.com/?p=36276)
- Automation annoyances: restockers refill before you relabel; can't be turned off per shelf. [FACT]
- Common tip: keep 2-3 spare boxes per product to cut reorder trips. [FACT] [guide](https://commonsensegamer.com/supermarket-simulator-tips-amp-tricks-for-beginners-hcfe/)
- From memory: you carry one box at a time, can drop boxes, and items snap onto shelves with a pop sound. [GUESS, unverified]

### TCG Card Shop Simulator
- Workers unlock at shop level 10, cost a fee, start next day. Early hires are slow; some players skip restock hires and restock early. Workers drop boxes at closing and can't do card tables. [FACT] [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/tips/worker-restocking-guide-in-tcg-card-shop-simulator), [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3070070/discussions/0/4849903793440095698)

### Gas Station Simulator
- Employees can take over restocking, cleaning messes, and payments. I found no complaint data. [FACT, thin] [guide](https://iota.msp.org/gas-station-simulator-when-do-you-get-employees-a-guide-to-hiring-help)

### PowerWash Simulator (the "satisfying chore" benchmark)
- Each object is split into parts with its own progress bar; finishing a part gives a chime and a little money. Low challenge, patient rhythm. [FACT] [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/reviews/powerwash-simulator-review-filthy-rich/1900-6417923/), [Geeks Under Grace](https://www.geeksundergrace.com/gaming/review-powerwash-simulator/)
- Constant water noise tires people, so it has audio sliders. Very long jobs wear out the novelty. [FACT]

### Pattern summary
| Works | Gets tedious |
|---|---|
| Clear progress (bars, full shelf) | Chore time much longer than the customer-drain time |
| Little reward sound on each finished piece | Long walks with one item |
| Chore ends with a visibly "done" state | Constant re-ordering |
| Staff arrive after you've mastered it | Staff that are slow or can't be controlled |

## Ideas for our game

**Step 4 (board rentals)**
- Rack holds a small fixed number of boards (start with 6 [GUESS]); carry one board at a time so the walk matters but stays short.
- Boards snap onto rack slots with a "thunk"; empty slot glows when a board returns. Return bin shows a count.
- Rack is placed close to the bin and the pool edge: keep a full restock loop under about 20 s [GUESS], so chore time is below renter turnover (the Supermarket complaint).
- Rental price: softboard cheap/free with a session ticket, premium boards paid. Start at about 1/5 of the session price [GUESS], tune after play.
- Show a small progress indicator ("Rack 4/6") plus a cha-ching when a rental is paid.
- Renters who find an empty rack get less patient (reuse Step 3 patience), so restocking has a clear reason.

**Later**
- Board sizes by skill (beginner needs big foamie; wrong board lowers happiness).
- Dinged boards: a returned board may be damaged; send it to a repair bay (small mini-task), lost boards charge a fee. Add deposit/ID mechanic.
- Wetsuits by size and season (cold-water pool days), with a drying rack.
- Wax-and-clean step before boards go back on the rack.
- Hired **rental clerk** unlocked at a shop level, with a visible cost, restocking only (no per-shelf surprises), and a switch to pause them.
- Shop retail: sunscreen, sunglasses, towels, fins; put towels near the counter. Order small at first.
- Upgrade: bigger rack or a board cart that carries 3 boards.
