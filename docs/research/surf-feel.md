# Surf feel: what makes surfing feel good (real life, games, spectating)
Date: 2026-10-20

Tags: **[S]** = from a source (linked). **[E]** = my estimate. Many sources here were blocked or thin (Patagonia, SurferToday and Seabreeze pages returned errors; the PLOS paper page was behind a browser check, so I only have its numbers from a search summary). I found NO source for g-forces in turns beyond one sensor log, NO measured barrel sound level, and NO reviews for True Surf. Say so before trusting any [E].
Body poses are already in [surfing-motion.md](surfing-motion.md); sound recipes in [sound-design.md](sound-design.md). This file is about FEEL: speed, camera, feedback, scoring, spectating.

## 1. What our code does today (read from the files)
- `PlayerSurfing.Ride`: you are glued to the wave: position = crest + `rideOffset` (0.3-5 m up/down the face) and `rideZ` (sideways). A/D set a sideways target speed of `carveSpeed` 7 m/s, reached at 14 m/s^2 (0.5 s). W/S move 1.5 m/s on the face. **There is no real "speed" the player builds**: forward speed is the wave's. `feel.Speed` (used for FOV) is just how fast you move over the ground.
- Camera: eye 0.6 paddling, 1.35 standing minus up to 0.45; roll up to 10 degrees, eased `Exp(-6)`; FOV +9 between 2 and 9 m/s; pop-up rise 0.8 s; landing squash. No shake, no spray on the lens, no sound change, no slow-mo.
- Scoring: `steer*20 + (3-rideOffset)*10` per second x10, +150 per air, +40/s at the end. Sponsor money = score/60 (max $50). No timing windows, no combo, no multiplier. Hint text is one line at the bottom ("A/D carve... Score: N").
- `RideFeel`: board lies on the face, leans by turn rate x speed, rail spray scales with lean squared, wake spray when speed > 1.5.
- `Surfer` (NPC): trick every 1.3-2 s, cheers via `Sfx.Play(Sound.Cheer)`, barrel only for Pros (`inBarrel`, offset 0.9), wipeout state. No crowd reaction beyond a cheer sound and a toast word.

## 2. Real surfing sensations
### Speed (numbers)

**Speed shape of a ride [S+E]:** drop = fast acceleration; bottom turn = the fastest point (compress low, then push out); the climb up the face trades speed for height; the top turn/snap = slowest, spray; then fall back down and speed up again. Trim (flat line high on the face) holds speed; pumping adds it. In a tube the surfer usually stalls or slows to stay deep, then speeds up to exit [E from the surfing-motion barrel notes].
**G-forces:** only data point is 1.77 g at a snap [S above]. A bottom turn at 8 m/s on a 10 m radius is 6.4 m/s^2 sideways, plus gravity, so about 1.2 g total [E, my physics]. Meaning: you feel pressed into the board and your legs get heavy, then light at the top of the turn.

### What surfers feel and notice
- **Takeoff push [E, from basic wave physics]:** as the wave lifts the board you feel a rising push from behind, then a sudden "falling" as the face steepens. Paddle hard 3-4 s before ([first waves](https://www.rapturecamps.com/learn-to-surf/surf-fundamentals/first-waves), already in surfing-motion.md), stop paddling when the wave takes you, then commit.
- **Commitment:** after the catch there is a point of no return: popping up late or hesitating pearls the nose. The pop-up is about 1.2 s in a lab ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7961430/)).
- **Reading the wave:** eyes scan 2-3 s ahead down the face; the steeper the angle the slower it peels, a flatter wave closes faster; a "closeout" gives only a second or two before it is all whitewash ([Rapture](https://www.rapturecamps.com/learn-to-surf/wave-knowledge/reading-waves), [Barefoot](https://barefootsurftravel.com/livemore-magazine/how-to-read-waves), via search summary). Surfers aim for the peak where the lip starts to pitch; too far out and you chase the shoulder with no power.
- **Barrel sound and light:** one Pipeline account says the crash noise drops away and things seem to slow down (Patagonia story, via search summary; the page itself was down). Another essay says it feels like time stopped ([Surfeurope](https://surfeuropemag.com/?p=14429) via search summary). Light looks green: the "green room" ([Inverse](https://inverse.com/article/6927-what-s-it-like-inside-a-barrel-wave)). A French surf site explains the "spit": air and water pushed out the tube's mouth in short bursts ([Surf-Report](https://www.surf-report.com/news/pedagogie-meteo/souffle-vague-vagues-pipeline-hawaii-teahupoo-1114197186.html)). Not found: any measured loudness. **My read [E]:** muffled roar (low-pass), your own breath, drips, dark edges, bright exit.
- **Wind and spray, chop chatter [E]:** wind noise rises with speed; spray hits chest/face on turns; on chop the board buzzes under the feet (fast small bumps, not big rolls). No source; this is common surf-film knowledge. Check by watching GoPro clips with Ido.
- **GoPro POV conventions [E + one forum]:** footage is shaky and the horizon tilts with the body; helmet cams look down the line, big head turns shake the picture ([Seabreeze thread](https://www.seabreeze.com.au/forums/Stand-Up-Paddle/SUP/Helmet-cam-and-image-stabilization--vid-)). Water droplets on the lens are the classic "this is real" cue.

| TransWorld Surf: Next Wave | variety of face, lip, air tricks; forgiving combo system; not deep but balances easy and hard | forgiving combos keep beginners happy | [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/transworld-surf-next-wave-review/1900-6024695/) |
| Kelly Slater's Pro Surfer | water looks and moves naturally; camera too close, hard to judge position; Tony-Hawk-like controls where stick direction decides bonus or wipeout | camera must show where you are on the wave | [GameSpot](https://gamespot.com/reviews/kelly-slaters-pro-surfer-review/1900-2880637/), [NWR](https://nintendoworldreport.com/review/3969/kelly-slaters-pro-surfer-gamecube) |

Not found: reviews of True Surf, Steep/Riders Republic, Descenders, or a shipped first-person or VR surf game. A USC lab VR surf prototype on a motion platform had almost no sickness in 17 people ([USC](https://magazine.viterbi.usc.edu/spring-2024/whats-next/riding-the-wave/)), but that used a moving platform.

**Patterns across them [S+E]:** (1) failure is the top complaint when it feels random or the camera hides the wave; (2) trick input is simple and the skill is in positioning/timing; (3) combos should be forgiving; (4) water reads best when it reacts to you (spray, wake).

### General game-feel tools
- **Skate's flick-it:** developers say it gives a tactile one-to-one link between stick and body ([EA](https://www.ea.com/ea-studios/ea-sports/news/skate-comes-full-circle)); critics said one stick for many moves was hard to execute ([Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/user-reviews/id/17835/a-good-game-but-at-times-flawed)). For us: mouse gestures could replace keys later, but keep keys first.
- **Tony Hawk combos:** manuals carry combos across flat ground, add multiplier, and a bail loses the whole combo while a normal landing banks points ([OSFTW](https://www.osftw.com/features/1547/how-the-manual-ruined-tony-hawks-pro-skater), [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/tony-hawks-pro-skater-1-2-remake-remaster-manual-how-to-guide)). Critique: stacking a multiplier with a safe move got cheesy. Lesson: bank-or-risk choice at the end; cap the multiplier.
- **Speed sensation (study):** wider FOV raises perceived speed; strong motion blur LOWERS it; higher camera altitude raised it ([Aalborg paper](https://eudl.eu/pdf/10.1007/978-3-319-55834-9_14), via search summary). Other guides: camera shake, FOV, sound, and near objects rushing past ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/how-to-make-game-characters-feel-lightning-fast)); fix control response before presentation; empty wide spaces feel slow because there is nothing to compare against ([iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/how-to-make-a-racing-game-feel-fast-and-responsive/)). Real speed also comes from wind, vibration and parallax which games lack ([Hargreaves](https://www.shawnhargreaves.com/blog/motion-blur.html)). So: skip motion blur, use FOV + wind sound + spray + rushing foam streaks.
- **Juice:** screen shake, hit pause ("sleep"), knockback from Vlambeer's talks ([summary](https://rpgplayground.com/research-making-a-juicy-game/)). Keep each effect short and decaying.

## 4. Making watching surfers exciting
Sources are thin: broadcast basics only. Slow motion replays mark big moments; a good replay picks one story, varies angles, and ramps into slow motion ([Football Life tips](https://zabbix.monitoring.qredits.adwise.nl/news/football-life-25-master-the), [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=421860)). Planet Coaster players complain guests do not react after a ride and that the camera is awkward ([Frontier forum](https://forums.frontier.co.uk/threads/guests-should-stop-and-admire-terrain-coasters-etc.483490)). So the gap in park sims is exactly "reaction to a great moment". Our version [E]:
- A highlight = tube ride, air, big combo, or a wipeout of a Pro. It fires: crowd cheer (louder with more spectators), a toast with the surfer's name, a short slow-mo (0.4x for 0.8 s) only if the player is within 25 m and looking at it, and a small money tip.
- Beach guests and idle surfers turn their heads and clap (reuse `BeachGuests`).
- Optional later: a "Highlights" photo/replay screen at day end showing the best moment.

## Ideas for our game (ranked, each one step)
Numbers marked [E] are starting points to tune by eye.

1. **Speed meter that pumping builds (Step 3 money loop, big payoff).** `PlayerSurfing`: add `float flow` 0..1. Riding high on the face (rideOffset < 1.5) and linking carves raises it: +0.25 per clean carve reversal (A then D within 1.2 s), +0.05/s trimming; it decays 0.15/s in the flats (rideOffset > 3). Flow multiplies `carveSpeed` (7 -> up to 9.5 m/s [E], matching 9-10 m/s pro peaks), the points rate (x1 to x2) and the FOV kick. Feeds `feel.Speed` or a new `Flow` property read by `UpdateCamera`. Show it as a thin bar in the HUD.
2. **Pop-up timing window with PERFECT / LATE (Step 3).** In `TryCatchWave`, compare the crest distance (`crest - x`, now accepted from -4 to +2 m) to the sweet spot: within +-0.6 m = "PERFECT" (+100 points, start `flow` at 0.4, extra spray burst), -4..-1.5 = "LATE" (start at 0, slower rise, `popUpStart` stretched), early = "EARLY" (small wobble). Toast via `Notifications.Post`. Keep the 2 s buffer for beginners.
3. **Camera shake and wind that scale with speed (Step 3).** `UpdateCamera`: add Perlin shake of 0.004 m position and 0.3 degrees roll at full flow, 2x on chop. Add a looping wind sound via `Sfx` whose volume and pitch follow `feel.Speed` (volume 0.1 -> 0.6, pitch 0.8 -> 1.3 [E]). No motion blur (study says strong blur reduces perceived speed).
4. **Barrel feel (Step 4+, with tube waves).** When the player (or later a tube wave) is under the lip: low-pass the music/ambience to about 1.2 kHz and add a low roar, dim the vignette to 35% darker, green tint, eye 0.9, water-drip droplets on the lens. At the exit, a short "spit" whoosh and flash of light. Needs a tube check in `PlayerSurfing.Ride` (e.g. `rideOffset < 0.8` while a wave breaks) and a post-processing volume weight in `WorldArt` settings. [E]
5. **Combo chain with a bank-or-risk end (Step 3).** Count tricks in a row (carve reversal, air, snap) within 4 s: multiplier x1, x1.5, x2, cap x3 (cap avoids the Tony Hawk cheese). Wipeout loses the unbanked combo; finishing the ride banks it. Show "x2 COMBO 340" text. Edit the score block in `Ride` and `TryAir`.
6. **Lens water and spray cam (Step 3).** A UI Toolkit overlay in the HUD (`Hud.uxml`, `Game.uss`) with 6-10 droplet sprites that appear when spray hits (hard carve, landing, wipeout) and fade over 1.5 s, driven by `PlayerSurfing` calling `GameHud` through a static event. Cheap and instantly says "water".
7. **Real wipeout feedback (Step 3).** `StartWipeout`: 0.12 s hit-pause (time scale 0.1 for 0.12 s, unscaled timer), camera kick of 8 degrees pitch, a muffled underwater low-pass for the 1.5 s and bubbles sound, then a bright "surface" gasp. Right now only a splash and a toast. Also make failure readable: tell WHY in the toast ("Too far down the face", "Fell off the back"); reviewers hate random-feeling failure.
8. **NPC highlight moments (Step 2 surfers / Step 4).** In `Surfer.DoTrick`, when a Pro gets "Tube ride!" or "AIR!": raise a static event `Highlights.Moment(position, strength)`. Listeners: `Sfx` crowd cheer scaled by nearby `BeachGuest` count, up to 5 guests within 15 m turn to look and clap, optional slow-mo of 0.4x for 0.8 s only if the player is within 25 m and the surfer is in the camera cone (dot > 0.7), plus a $5-15 tip popup. Never slow the clock for longer than 1 s (it is the whole game's time).
9. **Fewer, better NPC tricks and a speed shape (Step 2 polish).** `Surfer.trickInterval` 1.3-2 s -> 3-4 s for pros, 5+ s intermediate, rare for beginners (already in surfing-motion.md). Add a speed curve: faster down the face, slower at the top turn, so the spray and lean in `RideFeel` read more. Pump bob 1 Hz between tricks.
10. **Positioning matters (Step 3).** Like Championship Surfer: only allow an air when high on the face (already `rideOffset < 2`) AND with `flow` > 0.3; bottom-turn bonus when you go from high to low then back up within 1.5 s ("Bottom turn + snap" = +120). This gives mouse/W-S purpose beyond "stay high".
11. **Stronger carve feel (Step 3).** Raise `maxCameraRoll` 10 -> 14 on hard carves, add a 0.15 s head-look lead (yaw nudge of 3 degrees toward the turn), lower eye to 0.95 at the bottom of the turn then rise (compress/extend), `carveAcceleration` 14 -> 10 for a heavier, rail-pressure feel. Keep the horizon comfortable (reduce roll if the player turns on a "comfort" setting).
12. **Chop and trim vibration (Step 4+).** When `feel.Speed` is high and the face is bumpy: 25-30 Hz tiny camera jitter (0.002 m) and a faint buzz sound under the board; zero it during airs. Makes speed readable on flat water. [E]
13. **Crowd and ride-end moment (Step 3).** `EndRide`: instead of one toast, show a 2-second score card (score, best combo, top speed, longest tube) plus rating words (OK / Sick / EPIC). Cheer loudness follows the rating. Add a "top speed" stat to `GameStats`.
14. **Beginner mode (Step 3).** Auto-help for the first 3 rides: wider catch window (crest -5..+3), no wipeout from rideOffset until 4.8, a tutorial strip "Look down the line. Stay high." and a visible target ring for where to pop up. Reviewers praised forgiving combos and punished unforgiving physics.
15. **Highlights replay at day end (later).** Record the best moment (5 s of position/rotation of the rider) in `DayCycle.EndDay` summary and replay it with a slow-mo camera; only after #8 works.

Suggested order for sessions: (3 + 6 + 7) = "juice" session; (1 + 2 + 5 + 13) = "flow and score" session; (8 + 9) = "NPC excitement" session; 4 when tube waves exist.
