# Real surfing waves and what makes them different
Date: 2026-10-20

Sourcing: this file is mostly general surfing knowledge (not looked up source by source); numbers are rough. Breaking physics is in [wave-physics.md](wave-physics.md); pool tech in [surf-park-design.md](surf-park-design.md).

## Three kinds of break
| Type | Bottom | Behavior | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beach break | Sand, shifts with storms | Peaks pop up in different spots; short rides, often closeouts | Easy and friendly, unpredictable. Beginner pools copy this |
| Reef break | Rock or coral, fixed shape | Same spot, same shape every time; often steep and hollow | Powerful, dangerous if shallow. Barrel pools copy this |
| Point break | Land sticking out into the sea | Swell wraps the point and peels along it for a long way | Long, smooth rides (up to a minute or more). Ranch-style pools copy this |

## Famous waves
| Wave | Where | Type | What makes it special |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pipeline | Hawaii | Reef, shallow lava | Thick, hollow barrel; very shallow; mostly right; dangerous |
| Teahupo'o | Tahiti | Reef | Huge, thick lip, very heavy barrel; breaks in shallow reef; left |
| Jeffreys Bay | South Africa | Point | Super long fast right, many linked sections; great for turns |
| Malibu | California | Point (cobble) | Gentle, perfect small right; longboard classic |
| Snapper Rocks | Australia | Point (sand bar) | Fast long right, part of the "Superbank"; sand pumped to keep the shape |
| Uluwatu | Bali | Reef under a cliff | Several named sections, tubes and walls; lefts; tide matters |
| Mavericks | California | Reef, cold | Giant, heavy, cold; paddle-in big wave |
| Nazaré | Portugal | Canyon + beach | Huge waves because the deep underwater canyon focuses swell; tow-in |

## Wave quality words
- **Peeling:** breaks gradually along the wave so you can race along the face. Opposite of closeout.
- **Closeout:** the whole wave breaks at once; no ride (bad).
- **Mushy / soft:** slow wave with little power; hard to get speed; fine for beginners.
- **Hollow:** steep, tubes. **Fat:** wide and slow.
- **Sectiony:** breaks in chunks with flat gaps, you must speed up or slow down.
- **A-frame / peak:** a wave that splits in a triangle so one surfer goes left, one right.
- **Wedge:** a wave that bounces off a wall and joins the next, making a steep triangle bump.
- **Backwash:** water bouncing back off a wall or beach, bumping the next wave; messy.
- **Left / right:** direction as seen from the surfer, facing the beach.
- **Offshore wind** (blowing from land to sea): holds the face up, cleaner, more hollow. **Onshore** (sea to land): pushes the top down, choppy, mushy. **Glassy:** no wind, smooth.
- **Sets** and **period:** groups of bigger waves with lulls; longer period (more seconds between waves) = more power (see [wave-physics.md](wave-physics.md)).

## How pools imitate these
- **Beach break:** Cove inside zones and PerfectSwell soft shapes: small, forgiving, whitewater near shore.
- **Reef break:** a built concrete reef under the wave gives repeatable, hollow barrels (e.g., a Pipeline-style ramp).
- **Point break:** a long wave running beside a wall or along a diagonal bottom, as at the Ranch.
- **Wind:** indoor pools or sheltered lagoons are always "offshore-clean"; outdoor parks still lose sessions to wind and rain (see [wave-pools.md](wave-pools.md)).
- **Missing from pools:** random swell, tides, currents, big-wave scale.
